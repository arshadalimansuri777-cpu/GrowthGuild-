Questions

Before you buy

Is ad spend included in the Meta Ads plans?

No. Ad spend goes directly from you to Meta. Our fee covers setup, management and optimisation only.

Can I start with a single service and upgrade to a bundle later?

Yes. Most clients start with one service and move to a bundle. Your remaining period is adjusted in the bundle price.

How do payments work?

Projects: 50% advance to start, 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are prepaid at the start of each month.

What if I do not like the work?

Every plan carries a 7-day full refund guarantee within the first month. No questions asked.
