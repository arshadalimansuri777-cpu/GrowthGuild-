Questions

Before you buy

How do payments work?

50% advance to start, 50% when your website goes live.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain and hosting are paid by the client at actual cost, with no markup. If you want, we can arrange them for you.

What happens after the free support ends?

You can take our care plan at ₹1,499/month (updates, backup, small changes). An extra page is ₹799 and an extra revision round is ₹499.

Do you build websites for businesses in Nashik?

Yes. GrowthGuild is based in Nashik, Maharashtra, and builds websites for local shops, clinics, salons, gyms, coaching classes, restaurants, contractors, builders and showrooms.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every package includes basic SEO setup: page titles, meta descriptions and Google Search Console. The Growth and Premium packages also include Google Business Profile setup. No agency can honestly guarantee a number one ranking, because it depends on competition and your Google reviews.
